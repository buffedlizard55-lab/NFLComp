📖 Competition Methodology & Mathematical Foundations

Full documentation of statistical models, risk management parameters, execution slippage models, bankroll accounting, and no-hallucination compliance.

1. Zero-Lookahead Walk-Forward Backtesting

Every betting strategy operates in strict chronological sequence. When evaluating game $t$, models are provided only with information that existed prior to kickoff: rolling team ratings derived from games $1, \dots, t-1$, Friday afternoon injury status designations, opening-to-closing line movement, and pre-game NOAA weather forecasts. No future outcomes or post-game statistics are permitted to leak into decision timestamps.

2. FiveThirtyEight Calibrated Elo Model

Team power ratings are calibrated using dynamic margin-of-victory Elo:
P(Home Win) = 1 / (1 + 10^(-(Elo_Home - Elo_Away + HFA) / 400))
where $HFA \approx 48.0$ Elo points (~1.92 points of spread margin). Season-to-season regression reverts ratings 33% toward 1500 to account for offseason turnover.

3. Bivariate Poisson Joint Scoring Grid

Game score probability distributions are calculated across $0 \le x, y \le 60$ using independent offensive and defensive efficiency rates adjusted for home field and environmental drag:
P(Score_H = x, Score_A = y) = [ (λ_H^x e^(-λ_H) / x!) ] × [ (λ_A^y e^(-λ_A) / y!) ]

4. Execution Modeling, Fees & Slippage

Traditional sportsbook spreads and totals are modeled at verified closing market odds (-110 baseline with vig). Kalshi binary contracts include a $0.01 per-contract taker fee, realistic bid/ask spreads (2¢ to 5¢), and size-based slippage scaling above 100 contracts.

5. Zero-Hallucination Mandate

Every game, line, score, player injury status, and referee assignment is directly traceable to verified primary datasets (nflverse, FiveThirtyEight, NFLInjuryReport, NOAA). No hypothetical games or fabricated odds are permitted.
