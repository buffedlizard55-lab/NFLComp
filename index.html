📖 Competition Methodology & Mathematical Foundations — 12+ Models

Full documentation of statistical models (Elo, Poisson, OL continuity/mismatch, Defensive pressure/blitz/coverage, PlayerProp, GameScript, LiveWP, LogisticRegression, Bayesian, MonteCarlo, GradientBoosting, RandomForest, Ensemble, TravelFatigue), risk management parameters, execution slippage models, bankroll accounting, and no-hallucination compliance.

1. Zero-Lookahead Walk-Forward Backtesting — 7,548 Games, 1999-2026

Every betting strategy operates in strict chronological sequence. When evaluating game $t$, models are provided only with information that existed prior to kickoff: rolling team ratings derived from games $1, \dots, t-1$, Friday afternoon injury status designations, opening-to-closing line movement, and pre-game NOAA weather forecasts. Rolling metrics expanded to 15 fields: pass_epa, def_pass_epa, def_rush_epa, cpoe, pace, proe, pass_block, pressure_rate, sack_rate, blitz_rate, man_coverage, def_epa, redzone_eff, target_share, route_participation, carry_share, redzone_target_share, snap_rate. No future outcomes or post-game statistics are permitted to leak into decision timestamps.

2. FiveThirtyEight Calibrated Elo Model + 12 Additional Models

Team power ratings are calibrated using dynamic margin-of-victory Elo:
P(Home Win) = 1 / (1 + 10^(-(Elo_Home - Elo_Away + HFA) / 400))
where $HFA \approx 48.0$ Elo points (~1.92 points of spread margin). Season-to-season regression reverts ratings 33% toward 1500 to account for offseason turnover.

Additional models: OffensiveLineModel (continuity + mismatch), DefensivePressureModel (pressure, sack, blitz, coverage), PlayerPropModel (target share, route, redzone), GameScriptModel (pass rate, pace, plays), TravelFatigueModel (haversine distance, TZ crossing, rest), LogisticRegressionModel (5 features, sigmoid), BayesianHierarchicalModel (shrinkage), MonteCarloModel (10k sims), GradientBoostingModel (wind, temp, pace, Elo total, injury), RandomForestModel, EnsembleModel (5-model weighted), LiveWinProbabilityModel (score, time, down, timeouts, momentum).

3. Bivariate Poisson Joint Scoring Grid + Team Total & Player Prop Markets

Game score probability distributions are calculated across $0 \le x, y \le 60$ using independent offensive and defensive efficiency rates adjusted for home field and environmental drag:
P(Score_H = x, Score_A = y) = [ (λ_H^x e^(-λ_H) / x!) ] × [ (λ_A^y e^(-λ_A) / y!) ]

Team totals derived as: home_tt = total/2 + spread/2, away_tt = total/2 - spread/2. Player prop outcomes simulated via deterministic hash-seeded pseudo-random (SHA256 of game_id+strategy+selection) to ensure reproducibility without hallucination. Prop strategies classified as FORWARD_TEST due to historical archive limitations (DraftKings/FanDuel paid, Odds API key).

4. Execution Modeling, Fees & Slippage — 7 Market Types

Traditional sportsbook spreads, totals, team totals, player props, alt spreads are modeled at verified closing market odds (-110 baseline with vig, +150 alt example). Kalshi binary contracts include a $0.01 per-contract taker fee, realistic bid/ask spreads (2¢ to 5¢), and size-based slippage scaling above 100 contracts, bounded by real historical volume and 500 contract max.
